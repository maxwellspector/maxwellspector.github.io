Direction A · name as a monument

Spectrum

On this page, the name Spector, and the seven companies of a career in clean light, becomes a beam that splits into seven bands. The O in your name is the sun; as you scroll, its light passes through a prism (biology) and fans out into Janrain, Webtrends, Act-On, Conviva, Aurora, Omnidian and Anza.

Type

Hanken Grotesk
One family, medium weight, very tight.

Colour

Three stone greys. The spectrum is the only colour on the page.

Signature motion

A scroll-scrubbed refraction: the beam and the bands are tied to your scroll, so they rewind when you scroll back up.

Best if… / trade-off

You want something clever and quick to read. The trade-off is that it's light on photos unless we add an Off hours gallery.
Direction B · type-only statement, loud flat poster

Waterline

On this page, thirteen years on the Willamette and a 2007 conference title as a coach become a waterline that cuts your name in half, and a 2,000-metre race piece that is your career. Big poster type, flat primaries, and a stroke rate you can set.

Type

Anton + Work Sans
Condensed poster capitals, plain text.

Colour

Charcoal with flat primaries assigned to whole panels.

Signature motion

A masked headline rise, the bobbing waterline, and one global tempo set by stroke rate.

Best if… / trade-off

You want the most fun and personality. It reads more "person" than "executive", which may be what you want, or may not.
Direction C · the quiet instrument

Datasheet

On this page, the module datasheet, the document Anza's marketplace is built around, becomes a deadpan spec sheet for a product leader: rated output, operating environment, an I-V curve of a career, certifications and a warranty. Quiet, dark, exact, and funny once you notice.

Type

Albert Sans
Geometric, one family, small sizes doing the work.

Colour

Near-black, off-white, one deep crimson used three times on the page.

Signature motion

Slow and exact: lines rise, rows type in, and the curve draws itself. Nothing bounces.

Best if… / trade-off

You want recruiters and peers in clean energy to get the joke and respect it. The trade-off is that it's the least warm of the five.
Direction D · the pinned poster

Daylight

On this page, a career that moved toward the sun, from Janrain to Aurora Solar to Anza, becomes a day that passes as you scroll. Your name is pinned to two corners while the sun crosses between them, from dawn to noon (now) to night (off hours). The opening sky matches the real sun over Portland at the moment of the visit.

Type

Unbounded + Manrope
Very wide capitals, plain text.

Colour

Two tones that swap as day and night, with no third colour.

Signature motion

A pinned story: the name stays put across three screens while the sun and the cards move under it. Native scroll, nothing hijacked.

Best if… / trade-off

You want it to feel different every visit. It's the most motion-dependent, so the phone layout needs the most care.
Direction E · the contact sheet

Contact Sheet

On this page, 720,000 photographs and a biologist's lab notebook become a photographer's contact sheet with a loupe. Every chapter (each job, the boat, the brewery, the shop) is a strip of frames. Hover or drag the loupe to read a frame's story. This is the most photographic direction, so it needs your photos.

Type

Libre Franklin + Source Serif
Bold title, notebook serif.

Colour

Frost and slate, one teal. Your photographs bring the rest.

Signature motion

Prints develop into the ring on load, and the loupe follows your pointer smoothly. Otherwise calm.

Best if… / trade-off

You're willing to pick 30–60 of your photos. It's the richest and most browsable direction, and it does nothing without them.
