Biology first

Lewis & Clark College · OHSU

I started out working in molecular biology, first in a ribosome lab at Lewis & Clark, then at OHSU as a key contributor to a large stroke study, with three co-authored papers along the way.

At the same time I coached Lewis & Clark's novice men, easily the most fun job I've ever had. In 2007 we won the conference title.

Burnside BridgeNovember 2025. Now.

2025–now

Anza

Director of Platform Product Management

A two-sided marketplace for solar and energy-storage equipment, connecting the developers and utilities who buy it with the manufacturers who make it.

I lead the platform: the shared foundations every part of the marketplace is built on. I'm building a cross-cutting data architecture that matches the messy reality of the equipment world to what the software needs to support good decisions.

I defined how Anza builds product, on a few principles: earn conviction before you build, match rigor to risk, and measure outcomes rather than output. I stay close to our users on both sides of the market, and I helped lead the company's move to AI-first development.

I'm also responsible for the supplier experience: delivering value to the second side of our two-sided marketplace, from the deals themselves to everything that helps manufacturers reach and win buyers.
